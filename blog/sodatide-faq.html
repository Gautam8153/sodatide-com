How long before I notice anything?

Microbiome-focused synbiotics are generally used consistently for several weeks before people report noticeable changes in digestion and comfort. Give it a few weeks of daily use rather than judging it after a couple of days.

Are there side effects?

Most people tolerate synbiotic formulas well. Some notice mild gas or bloating in the first days as the gut adjusts to added fibers and cultures; this usually settles. If you have a medical condition or take medication, check with your doctor first.
